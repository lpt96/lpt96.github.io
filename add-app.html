Folder structure this site expects:

home-screen.html, data.json, contact.html, privacy-policies.html, and add-app.html sit at the top level, alongside two subfolders:

  • Logos/ — app and extension logo images
  • Privacy Policies/ — privacy policy PDFs
How to use this with data.json:
  1. Fill in the fields above — the box below updates as you type.
  2. Click Copy to clipboard.
  3. Open data.json in any text editor.
  4. Find the closing ] at the very end of the file. Just before it, after the last } of the previous entry, add a comma , — then paste your new block on its own line before the final ].
  5. Save the file and re-upload it (or push it) alongside your site. The new app will appear automatically, grouped under its category.
About the logo field:

Save the app's logo image into the Logos folder, then just type the filename into the Logo field above (e.g. flexle.png — no need to include "Logos/", it's added automatically). Leave it blank to use the 2-letter initials tile instead — that fallback also applies automatically if a logo file is ever missing or renamed.

About the privacy policy field:

Each app has its own bespoke privacy policy — you create and upload the PDF for it yourself. Once you've saved that PDF into the Privacy Policies folder, just type its filename into the field above (e.g. flexle-privacy-policy.pdf — no need to include "Privacy Policies/", it's added automatically).
